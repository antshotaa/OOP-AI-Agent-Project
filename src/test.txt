PANDORA PARADOXXX was added to maimai FiNALE on May 10th, 2019, as the final song of PANDORA BOXXX.
When entering the song from PANDORA BOXXX, a 15-second video based on the video of the song is played, in which the title and artist is revealed, as seen in the Re:MASTER chart video on this page.
Its Re:MASTER chart was added on the same day.
Since maimai でらっくす, PANDORA PARADOXXX is available by default.
In Asian English versions of maimai, PANDORA PARADOXXX's artist is romanised as Sakuzyo.
In PANDORA BOXXX, PANDORA PARADOXXX's title is stylized as PΛNDΘRΛ PΛRΛDΘXXX.
Upon release, PANDORA PARADOXXX broke the record of the highest maimai level in all 6 difficulties, a record it still currently holds alongside Xaleid◆scopiX as of maimai でらっくす CiRCLE, as well as the highest number of notes in MASTER and Re:MASTER difficulties.
PANDORA PARADOXXX's MASTER and Re:MASTER charts were respectively the first level 13+ and 14 charts in the 14-level scale.
In the 15-level scale introduced in maimai でらっくす PLUS, the Re:MASTER chart of PANDORA PARADOXXX was the only level 15 chart until the release of 系ぎて in maimai でらっくす BUDDiES.
PANDORA PARADOXXX held the highest internal level for charts in all difficulties until maimai でらっくす PRiSM PLUS.
PANDORA PARADOXXX still holds the record for the 4 difficulties excluding BASIC. In particular, its internal levels for ADVANCED and EXPERT charts are not tied with any other song.
At the time of its release, the maimai Re:MASTER chart of PANDORA PARADOXXX had the highest notecount among all non-宴会場 charts, and the third-highest overall, only behind 疾走あんさんぶる 宴 協 and FREEDOM DiVE (tpz Overcute Remix) 宴 狂.
No one unlocked the song on the first day in maimai, which didn't happen since Panopticon. No one unlocked the song even in the entire LIFE5 period; the highest achievement rate anyone got was 60%. The Re:MASTER chart was even harder; the first person who unlocked the chart did it at LIFE50.
PANDORA PARADOXXX contains many BPM changes and time signature changes, both of which are rare in maimai
